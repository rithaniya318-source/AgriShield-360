Why soil testing matters

Soil tests can measure pH and nutrient levels such as nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium (NPK).

  • Nitrogen supports leafy growth.
  • Phosphorus supports root development.
  • Potassium supports overall plant health.
  • Organic matter improves soil structure.

Do not select a fertilizer dose based only on leaf colour or this demo. Use a soil-test report and local agricultural expert advice.

04 / WASTE TO WEALTH

Turn Farm Waste into Fertility

Some everyday organic leftovers can become useful compost when managed properly.

🧅

Onion peels

Add small amounts to a compost pile. Mix with dry leaves and other organic material. Compost fully before applying.

🥕

Vegetable scraps

Fruit and vegetable scraps can be composted. Chop them into smaller pieces and cover with dry material.

🍂

Dry leaves

Dry leaves provide carbon-rich material. Mix them with moist green waste to balance the compost pile.

🌾

Crop residues

Healthy crop residues can be composted or mulched where appropriate. Avoid spreading diseased plant material.

Composting tip: keep the pile moist but not waterlogged, allow air circulation, and let it decompose fully. Avoid meat, dairy, oily food and chemically contaminated waste. Do not place fresh scraps directly around plant stems.
